Good to know

Questions, answered

Is it my first Brazilian wax. Will it hurt?

There's a quick sting, then it settles. Your therapist will talk you through it, work fast and use calming aftercare. Each wax usually feels easier than the last.

How long should my hair be before waxing?

About a quarter of an inch (around 2–3 weeks of growth after shaving) so the wax can grip properly. If it's longer, we'll trim it first.

What is a vajacial?

A facial for the bikini area. It cleanses, gently exfoliates and soothes after waxing to help prevent ingrown hairs and dark spots. It's the perfect add-on to a Brazilian.

Any aftercare tips?

For 24 hours, skip hot baths, sauna, the gym and tight clothing. After 48 hours, exfoliate gently a few times a week to keep ingrown hairs away.
